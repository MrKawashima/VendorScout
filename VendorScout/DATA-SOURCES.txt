Source generation date: 2026-11-22; schema 1.9; target Forever 1.60.1.

The bundled database contains the COMPLETE union of npc.sells and item.vendors
in this source snapshot, including vendor names, item names, stock limits,
faction reactions, item restrictions, spawn locations and nine available name
translations. The source's Horde variant is reconciled against both indexes.
It is a vendor-focused extract, not a copy of unrelated quests, drops or recipes.

All 18,010 source relationships are retained. Items: 3,940; vendors: 1,151.
Base profile locations: 1,210; mapped positions: 1,186; vendors with no mapped
position: 39. One vendor has faction-specific spawn overrides.

Coordinates are percentages of the target Forever maps in the source; they are
normalized to 0..1 and mapped from AreaTable IDs to UiMapIDs. Invalid/sentinel
coordinates stay unknown. They are never replaced with a zone centre.

Names and relations can be incomplete, outdated, conditional or inherited from
Classic. NPC and item indexes can disagree; their union preserves available
evidence, not proof that a purchase is available now. Class ID zero is omitted
by the source and treated as Consumable. A zero stock limit means unlimited OR
unknown, so the UI labels it as unknown unless locally observed.

The full downloaded input files needed to reproduce this extract, their SHA-256
hashes, the pinned source identifier, the compiler and tests are provided in
VendorScout-1.0.0-source.zip. The addon itself requires no external addon.
